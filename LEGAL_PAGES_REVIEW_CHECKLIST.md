# Legal Pages Review Checklist

The new `/privacy/` and `/terms/` pages are deliberately marked as drafts. Do not merge or rely on them as final legal documents until the following items have been reviewed and approved.

## Business details

- [ ] Confirm the legal business name and operating entity.
- [ ] Confirm the approved disclosure:
  ```text
  thefunders.ca operates under BRX Mortgage Inc., FSRA Licence #13463.
  ```
- [ ] Add a mailing address and named privacy contact / privacy officer if required.
- [ ] Confirm the support contact email and phone number.

## Privacy / data handling

- [ ] Confirm all data collected by the public report-builder.
- [ ] Confirm public report-link behavior, access controls, and expiry plan.
- [ ] Confirm Netlify Blobs, Supabase, HubSpot, email, MLS/property lookup, analytics, and other service providers.
- [ ] Define retention and deletion periods.
- [ ] Confirm consent wording for report requests, marketing, Realtor updates, and rewards.
- [ ] Confirm cross-border processing and Canadian privacy-law requirements.

## Report / financial content

- [ ] Confirm rate-source and rate-update process.
- [ ] Confirm insured versus conventional/uninsured scenario controls.
- [ ] Confirm report calculations, closing-cost assumptions, rental estimates, and property-data sourcing.
- [ ] Confirm disclaimer language around estimates, lender decisions, and personalized advice.

## Rewards programme

- [ ] Confirm eligibility, verification, approval, cancellation, and fulfilment terms.
- [ ] Confirm travel/ticket restrictions, taxes, reward value, and availability language.
- [ ] Confirm brokerage, referral-compensation, advertising, and regulatory requirements.

## Website release

- [ ] Review draft Terms and Privacy pages with legal/compliance counsel.
- [ ] Replace the draft banner with an approved effective-date statement.
- [ ] Confirm `/terms/` and `/privacy/` links work from the report-builder and footer.
- [ ] Test mobile, accessibility, and browser print behavior.
