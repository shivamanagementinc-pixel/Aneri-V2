title: Financing Questions? We have answers.
description: Everything you need to know before starting your homeownership journey.
items:
  - question: What is the minimum down payment in Canada?
    answer: For homes under $500,000, the minimum down payment is 5%. Between
      $500,000 and $999,999, it is 5% on the first $500,000 and 10% on the
      remainder. Homes $1 million and above require 20% down.
  - question: Can newcomers to Canada get a mortgage?
    answer: Yes. Many Canadian lenders offer newcomer mortgage programs that accept
      limited Canadian credit history, foreign income, and international assets.
      We help newcomers navigate these specialized options.
  - question: How long does a formal mortgage review take?
    answer: Timing depends on the information available, lender requirements, and
      the buyer's situation. The Funders Team will explain what is needed for
      review and keep you informed as the file progresses. A property scenario
      or report is not a pre-approval or lender decision.
  - question: Do you charge fees for your services?
    answer: For most standard residential mortgages, our services are free to you —
      we are compensated by the lender. In some complex situations, a fee may
      apply, which we will always disclose upfront.
  - question: What is the difference between fixed and variable rates?
    answer: A fixed rate stays the same for your entire term, giving you predictable
      payments. A variable rate fluctuates with the prime rate, which can mean
      lower costs when rates drop but higher costs when they rise. We will help
      you decide which fits your risk tolerance.
  - question: Can I get a mortgage if I am self-employed?
    answer: Absolutely. We work with lenders who understand self-employed income,
      including those who accept stated income or alternative documentation.
      Your business does not disqualify you from homeownership.
